Description
Established in 1965, the role of he Raleigh Fine Arts Society, according to its website, is to To cultivate, promote, and engage in the arts. This collection ranges from 1965-2022 and contains administrative records, exhibition and contest files, scrapbooks, and awards.
Description
This collection consists of the records of the Raleigh Fine Arts Society. Included in this collection are administrative records, articles of incorporation, charters, by-laws, meeting minutes, annual reports, presidents' speeches, correspondence, financial records, yearbooks, handbooks, membership directories, presidents workbooks, and recording secretaries notebooks, as well as records documenting the planning and execution of the organizations various community projects. This collection also includes documents related to the organization's annual events such as the North Carolina Artists' Exhibition, the Literary Contest, and the Choral Celebration. Scrapbooks in this collection contain photographs, newspaper clippings, and other items documenting the history, events, activities, and projects of the Raleigh Fine Arts Society. Additionally, trophies, plaques, and certificates awarded to the Raleigh Fine Arts society are present as well. This collection is arranged in the following series: (1) Administrative Records, 1965-2022 (2) Project Files, 1971-1997 (3) Exhibit and Contest Files, 1965-2022 (4) Scrapbooks, 1965-1992; 1997-1998 (5) Awards, 1988; 1994; 2013; 2014 .
Description
The Administrative Records, 1965-2022 series contains administrative records, financial records, correspondence, annual reports, committee records, Presidents Workbooks, Recording Secretary Notebooks, yearbooks, membership records, articles of incorporation, and by-laws. Administrative records, President's Workbooks, and Recording Secretary's Notebooks are arranged chronologically by year and by the name of the President or Recording Secretary holding the position at the time.
Description
The Project Files, 1971-1997 series contains records documenting various community projects, such as the restoration of the carousel in Pullen Park, the Light in Color project, the creation of Raleigh: A Guide to NCS Capital Bicentennial Edition 1991-1997 and Raleigh: A Guide to NCs Capital 1975-1976, and documents regarding the placement of docents and volunteers at the North Carolina Museum of Art, the North Carolina Museum of History, the North Carolina Symphony, and Executive Mansion. Also included are Task Force Committee records.
Description

The following scrapbooks were returned to the Raleigh Fine Arts Society in May of 2016 and are no longer included in this collection. Volume 2 Volume 4 Volume 9 Volume 11 Volume 12 Volume 13

Biographical or Historical Data
The Raleigh Fine Arts Society was created as the result of an art exhibition held in the basement of the original Olivia Raney Library building in August, 1964. A group of volunteers who helped organize and present the exhibit recognized the need for a formal organization dedicated to supporting artists and arts-related activities in Wake County. The Raleigh Fine Arts Society was created in June 1965 and was granted its charter the following month. According to the organizations website, The Raleigh Fine Arts Society seeks to encourage public appreciation, enthusiasm, support, and understanding of the arts in order to further the growth and enhance the quality of the arts in the community, as we have done since our founding in 1965. To accomplish this goal, the Raleigh Fine Arts Society organizes their own community projects such as the annual North Carolina Artists Exhibition, the annual Literary Contest for high school students, and the annual Choral Celebration for elementary students. the organization also sponsors other arts-related exhibitions and events in the community. Members of the Raleigh Fine Arts Society also volunteer at local museums, art galleries, exhibitions, and events, and serve on arts-related committees and boards in the city, county, and state. The Raleigh Fine Arts Society has received several awards, including the Raleigh Medal of Arts Award, in 1989 and 1995 and the Paladin Award in 2001, awarded by the Greater Raleigh Chamber of Commerce and the United Arts Council of Raleigh and Wake County. In 2014, the organization was inducted into the Raleigh Hall of Fame.
