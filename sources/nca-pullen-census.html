Pullen Park
Raleigh, NC
Park:Pullen Park
Description:W. H. Dentzel (#106 Catalog Style)
Carousel Class:Classic Wood Carousel
Last Update:2021
Status:Active
Model:#106
Year Built:1912
Type:3 rows, Park, All Wood composition
Figures:19 Jumping Horses, 10 Standing Horses, 23 Menagerie Animals (4 Cats, 1 Deer, 1 Mule, 4 Ostriches, 4 Pigs, 4 Rabbits, 2 Giraffes, 1 Tiger, 1 Lion, 1 Goat), 2 chariots
Music:Band Organ: 1924 Wurl 125 #3728
Notes: Placed on the National Register of Historic Places - 1976, Received the NCA Historic Carousel Award - 2015
Comments:Original paint conserved beneath new during restoration. Bi-level platform with two large 3 seat chariots. A new building completed in 2011.
History:Bloomsbury Park, Raleigh, NC, 1912 to 1921
Pullen Park, Raleigh, NC, 1921 to present
